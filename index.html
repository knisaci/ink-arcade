What is leverage?
Leverage multiplies your position size. With 5x leverage and 1,000 credits, you control a 5,000 credit position. Profits and losses are both amplified — a 10% price move means a 50% gain or loss on your collateral.
What is a liquidation price?
If the price moves too far against you, the protocol closes your position automatically to prevent losses beyond your collateral. That price is called the liquidation price. For a long: entry × (leverage-1) / leverage. For a short: entry × (leverage+1) / leverage.
What are game credits?
Game credits are not real money. They are a scoring system so you can see PnL in realistic numbers without any financial risk. The math is real — only the stakes are not.
How to play
1 Pick a market scenario — each has a fixed entry price, exit price, and max leverage.
2 Choose Long or Short, set your leverage and size, then guess the liquidation price. Use the formula shown — or see if you can figure it out yourself.
3 Open your position on-chain, then resolve it. The contract calculates your PnL and whether you were liquidated — all verifiable on Ink Explorer.
