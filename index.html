Tydro Lending Simulator
Supply collateral. Borrow against it. Survive a price drop — or get liquidated.
LENDING
What is collateral?
Collateral is the asset you deposit to back a loan. On Tydro, you supply ETH or BTC and borrow USDC against it. If your collateral value drops too much, your position gets liquidated.
What is a health factor?
Your health factor shows how safe your position is. Above 1.0 = safe. Below 1.0 = liquidated. The higher it is, the more price can drop before you get liquidated.
What is LTV?
LTV (Loan to Value) is how much you can borrow relative to your collateral. 75% LTV means you can borrow up to $750 for every $1,000 of collateral. Borrowing close to the max increases liquidation risk.
How to play
1 Pick a lending scenario — each has a different asset, LTV, and liquidation threshold.
2 Supply collateral and borrow against it. Your health factor shows how safe you are.
3 Simulate a price drop. The contract calculates whether you survive or get liquidated — all on-chain.
