PERP DEX
What is leverage?
Leverage multiplies your position. With 5x and 1,000 credits, you control 5,000 credits. Gains and losses are both amplified — a 10% move means 50% gain or loss on your collateral.
What is a liquidation price?
If price moves too far against you, the protocol closes your position automatically. That price is your liquidation price — shown in gold on the next screen.
What are game credits?
Not real money. A scoring system so you can see realistic PnL numbers without financial risk. The math is real — only the stakes are not.
1Pick a market scenario.
2Choose direction, leverage, size. Your liquidation price is shown — enter your guess within 5%.

Tydro Lending Simulator
Supply collateral. Borrow against it.
Survive a price drop — or get liquidated.
LENDING
What is collateral?
The asset you deposit to back a loan. You supply ETH or BTC and borrow USDC against it. If your collateral value drops too much, you get liquidated.
What is a health factor?
Shows how safe your position is. Above 1.0x = safe. Below 1.0x = liquidated. Higher is safer — more room for the price to drop before you get liquidated.
What is LTV?
Loan to Value — how much you can borrow vs your collateral. 75% LTV means you can borrow up to $750 for every $1,000 supplied. Borrowing near the max is risky.
